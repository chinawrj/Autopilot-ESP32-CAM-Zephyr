## Architecture Decisions

- **Manual SCCB probe first**: Skip Zephyr's ov2640.c driver initially — it requires
  CONFIG_VIDEO which adds ~2MB static heap, and our DRAM is at 94.77%.
- **SYS_INIT for camera bootstrap**: XCLK (LEDC PWM) and PWDN (GPIO) must be
  initialized before any I2C communication with the camera.
- **SRAM DMA buffers**: Original ESP32 cannot DMA directly to PSRAM from I2S.
  Use SRAM bounce buffers + CPU copy to PSRAM.
- **Standalone capture module**: Custom I2S camera driver feeding frame_source
  abstraction, not full Zephyr video API.
